FAQ

Internship questions

Do I need previous experience?

Not for every track. Several tracks are beginner-friendly. Choose the level that fits you and your mentor will help you grow from there.

Can I apply for more than one track?

Yes. Submit a separate application for each track you're interested in and mention your preferred one in your message.

How much time will I need each week?

Time commitments are agreed with each intern when they join, so the schedule can fit alongside studies or work.

What happens after I apply?

We review your details and get in touch to arrange a short conversation about your goals and the best track for you.
