FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about SpireX.

Who can join SpireX Foundation?

Anyone motivated to learn practical skills is welcome — from complete beginners to early-career professionals looking to grow.

Do I need to know how to code already?

No. Several programs and internship tracks are designed for beginners, and mentors help you progress step by step.

How do I apply for an internship?

Visit the Internships page, choose a track and complete the short application form.

Can I become a mentor or partner with SpireX?

Yes! Select "Mentoring or partnership" in the form above and tell us how you'd like to get involved.

Where can I learn about your programs?

Visit our Programs page to explore current learning options.

Can beginners join SpireX?

Yes, several programs and internship tracks are designed for beginners.

When can I expect a reply?

We aim to reply to direct email inquiries within 2 business days.

Not sure where to begin?

Browse our programs and internships to see which path fits you best.
